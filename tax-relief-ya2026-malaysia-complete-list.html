Malaysia Tax Relief YA2026: The Complete List for Freelancers

Why reliefs matter more when you're self-employed

Personal tax reliefs reduce your chargeable income before LHDN's progressive tax bands are applied — which means every ringgit of relief you claim lowers your tax at your marginal rate, not just by a flat amount. Salaried employees are often reminded about reliefs by HR at year-end; freelancers and sole proprietors usually are not, which is why reliefs tend to be under-claimed in this group. Here is the full set relevant to a typical Malaysian freelancer.

Automatic relief

Every Malaysian tax resident receives an individual relief of RM9,000 automatically, with no action required and no receipts to keep. This is the baseline every other relief sits on top of.

Reliefs tied to family and care

Parent relief covers medical treatment, care, and support for a qualifying parent, capped at RM8,000 combined. Child relief is available per unmarried child, with a higher cap for children in full-time higher education — the exact figures are published in LHDN's current schedule and should be checked each year, since they are periodically revised.

Reliefs tied to savings and insurance

Self-employed individuals can claim relief on voluntary EPF contributions (including the i-Saraan scheme), typically capped at RM4,000, and separately on approved life insurance or family takaful premiums, typically capped at RM3,000. Education and medical insurance premiums (covering self, spouse, or child) usually carry their own separate cap, often around RM3,000. A net deposit into a child's SSPN education savings account can also attract relief, commonly capped around RM8,000, and contributions to a Private Retirement Scheme (PRS) or an approved deferred annuity typically have a further cap, often around RM3,000.

Lifestyle and education reliefs

Lifestyle relief covers everyday items like books, approved gadgets, internet subscriptions, and gym or sports equipment, generally capped around RM2,500. Separately, fees for approved courses, professional certifications, or postgraduate study can qualify for an education relief, generally capped around RM7,000. Because these caps and category definitions are reviewed in most Budget announcements, always check the current Year of Assessment figures on LHDN's own site rather than assuming last year's caps still apply.

How reliefs reduce your tax, step by step

Start with your net business profit (income minus allowable expenses), subtract every relief you are entitled to claim, and the resulting figure is your chargeable income — the number LHDN's progressive bands are actually applied to. A freelancer who claims RM9,000 individual relief plus a further RM10,000–15,000 across the categories above can meaningfully shift which tax band their income falls into, which is why reviewing this list every year, rather than defaulting to the same few reliefs, is worth the few minutes it takes.
